How to navigate

1

Look around — drag to pan on desktop, tilt your device on mobile, or simply move your head in VR.

2

Find a waypoint marker — white circles sit on the floor where each nearby location is, just like a street-view walk-through.

3

Gaze to travel — click or tap a circle (or hold your gaze on it for ~2 seconds in VR). You'll fade and arrive there.

4

On Quest 3: press the VR button, point your right controller at a marker and press trigger — or just use gaze.

Best experienced with headphones  ·  Requires a modern browser with WebGL
